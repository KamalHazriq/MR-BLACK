The Mr. Black Game

The social deduction word game for friends — everyone shares a secret word, except the one who gets nothing at all. Up to 20 players, each on their own phone. No app, no signup.

A word game where one of you is flying blind

Most players are Civilians and share one secret word. One or more are Undercover and get a word that's close, but not the same. And at least one is Mr. Black, who gets no word at all and has to bluff from nothing.

Each round everyone gives a single clue about their word. Then the table argues, votes, and eliminates whoever looks most suspicious. Civilians win by rooting out every impostor; the impostors win by surviving until they match the Civilians in number.

Playing apart, not passing a phone

This version is built for friends who aren't in the same room. Everyone opens the same link on their own device, so your role and word only ever appear on your screen. Get on a voice call for the arguing, and let the app handle roles, turn order, votes, and the log of who said what.

Strategy, by role

  • Civilian: be specific enough that other Civilians know you're one of them, vague enough that Mr. Black can't steal the word.
  • Undercover: you have a real word, just the wrong one. Listen first, then bend your clue toward the group.
  • Mr. Black: you have nothing. Read the room, give a clue generic enough to pass, and save your guess for when you're caught.

How to play

Five steps. Your own screen.

No passing a phone around — everyone joins with a room code and plays from their own device.

01
Roles

Civilians share a word. The Undercover gets a near-miss. Mr. Black gets nothing at all.

02
Join

The host creates a room and shares the code or invite link. Your role shows only on your screen.

03
Clues

Take turns typing one short clue about your word. Everything lands in a shared log.

04
Vote

Argue it out, then everyone votes at once. The most-voted player is out, and their role is revealed.

05
Win

Civilians win by catching every impostor. A caught Mr. Black gets one guess at the word to steal it back.

FAQ

Questions about Mr. Black

Do we need an app or an account?

No. Everyone opens the link in a browser. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for.

How many people can play?

Up to 20 in one room. Five to ten is the sweet spot for a fast, tense game.

Do we need to be in the same room?

No — it's built for playing apart. Use a voice call for the talking; the app handles roles, turns, votes and the log.

What does Mr. Black do?

Mr. Black gets no word and has to bluff every round. If caught, they get one guess at the Civilian word — get it right and the impostors steal the round.

What's the difference between Undercover and Mr. Black?

Undercover has a real word, just a slightly different one. Mr. Black is bluffing completely blind.

Can we use our own words?

Yes. The host can add custom pairs in the lobby, pick which categories are in play, or switch to Custom mode and use only your own words.

Is my word visible to anyone else?

No. Your role and word appear only on your device, behind a tap-to-reveal.

What if I get disconnected?

Reopen the link and you drop straight back into your seat with your role intact, as long as the room is still alive.

      FAQ

      Do we need an app or an account?

      No. Everyone just opens the link in a browser. There's nothing to install and nothing to sign up for.

      How many people can play?

      Up to 20 in one room. The sweet spot for a fast, tense game is usually 5–10.

      Do we need to be in the same room?

      No — this version is built for playing apart. Hop on a voice call (Discord, FaceTime, whatever) for the talking, and use the app for roles, turns, votes, and the log.

      What does Mr. Black do?

      Mr. Black gets no word at all and has to bluff a plausible clue every round. If caught, they get one final guess at the Civilian word — guess it and the impostors steal the round.

      What's the difference between Undercover and Mr. Black?

      Undercover has a real word — just a slightly different one from the Civilians, so they can bluff off actual knowledge. Mr. Black has no word at all and is bluffing completely blind.

      Can we use our own words?

      Yes. In the lobby, the host can open the Words section and add custom pairs, or untick any of the built-in pairs to take them out of the draw.

      Is my word visible to anyone else?

      No. Your role and word only ever appear on your own device, behind a tap-to-reveal, and are never shown to other players.

      What happens if I get disconnected?

      Just reopen the link — you'll drop right back into your seat with your role and the game state intact, as long as the room is still active.
